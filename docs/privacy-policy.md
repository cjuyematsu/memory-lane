# PastPic Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

PastPic is built so that your data stays yours. This policy is short because the app collects almost nothing.

## The short version

Everything PastPic does happens on your phone. Your photos, your location, and everything the app derives from them are processed entirely on-device and never leave your device. PastPic has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own.

## What the app accesses, and why

- **Photos.** PastPic reads your photo library, including the location and date metadata saved in your photos, to build its memories feed, the Near Me view, and place-based notifications. This index is stored only on your device.
- **Location.** PastPic uses your location to show photos taken near you and, if you enable memory notifications, to run geofences around places where you took photos long ago. If you grant "Always" location, it is used only so those notifications can arrive while the app is closed. Your location is never logged, stored historically, or transmitted.
- **Camera.** Used only for the Retakes feature, to re-take an old photo from the same spot. Captures are saved only where you choose.
- **Notifications.** Used to deliver memory notifications you have enabled.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings; the app will keep working with reduced features.

## The one exception: crash reports

If PastPic crashes, an anonymous crash report (a stack trace and basic device information such as the device model and OS version) is sent to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so the problem can be fixed. Crash reports contain no photos, no location data, no personal information, and no identifiers linked to you.

## What PastPic does not do

- No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
- No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
- No account creation; the app never asks for your name, email, or any personal details.

## Children

PastPic does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
